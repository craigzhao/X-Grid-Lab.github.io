The University of Texas at Arlington announced that Prof. Tianqiao Zhao is participating in a Brookhaven National Laboratory-led project supported through Genesis Mission Phase II.

The collaboration is developing a next-generation grid foundation model to accelerate evaluation of new electric-grid loads and planning scenarios. UTA's contribution focuses on agentic AI workflows, connecting model capabilities with specific applications, and incorporating grid validation in the loop.

The project brings together AI model development, application-layer tools, and utility engagement.
