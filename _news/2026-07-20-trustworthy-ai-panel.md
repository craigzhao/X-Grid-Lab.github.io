Prof. Tianqiao Zhao contributed to the IEEE PES General Meeting panel **From Physics-Informed Neural Networks to Trustworthy AI: Certified, Fast, and Resilient AI for the Future Power Grid** in Montreal, Canada.

The panel examined how physical constraints, verification, and optimization can help learning methods support dependable grid analysis and control. It brought together perspectives on dynamic models, stability assessment, and engineering decisions.
