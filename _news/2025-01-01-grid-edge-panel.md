Prof. Tianqiao Zhao chaired **Empowering the Grid Edge: Innovations, Integration, and Cybersecurity in the New Energy Landscape** at the 2025 IEEE PES Grid Edge Technologies Conference and Exposition in San Diego, California.

The session brought together perspectives on emerging grid-edge technologies and their integration with secure energy systems.
