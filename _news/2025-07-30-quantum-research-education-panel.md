Prof. Tianqiao Zhao contributed to the IEEE PES General Meeting panel **Opportunities and Challenges in Research and Education of Quantum Computing for Power Systems**, held in Austin, Texas.

The discussion connected quantum methods with power-system applications and considered the research, education, and workforce needs involved in developing these capabilities.
