Tianqiao Zhao participated as a speaker in the Resilience Week session on **Understanding Critical Infrastructure Systems and Interdependencies**, held in Washington, DC.

The session brought together researchers considering how connections among infrastructure systems affect resilience. Zhao participated while at Brookhaven National Laboratory.
