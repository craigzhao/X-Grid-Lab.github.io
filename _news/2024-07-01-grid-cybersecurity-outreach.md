During Brookhaven National Laboratory's summer 2024 energy-workforce program, Tianqiao Zhao gave high-school students a presentation on grid cybersecurity.

The program introduced students to energy research and career pathways through scientist presentations, hands-on activities, and facility visits. Zhao participated as a guest speaker while working at Brookhaven.
