Prof. Tianqiao Zhao serves as **Secretary** of the IEEE PES Task Force on Reinforcement Learning for Power System Dynamic Controls, within the Working Group on Machine Learning for Power Systems. The task force brings together researchers working on learning methods for power-system dynamics and control.

He has also served as **Co-Chair** of the IEEE PES Task Force on Empowering the Future of Power Systems Operation and Control with Quantum Computing. This work connects quantum-computing research with power-system operation and control.
