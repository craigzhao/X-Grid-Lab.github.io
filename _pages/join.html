Ph.D. research opportunities

We welcome creative, motivated, and open-minded students from electrical engineering, computer science, and related disciplines. Our research connects power engineering with machine learning, optimization, and quantum information science.

Areas you could explore

  • Physics-aware and trustworthy AI for power and energy systems
  • Renewable forecasting, uncertainty, and extreme-weather resilience
  • Resilient optimization and risk-aware planning and operation
  • Cyber-physical security and anomaly detection
  • Quantum computing and hybrid quantum-classical learning
  • Quantum communication for future grid infrastructure

Undergraduate & master's research

We welcome students interested in building real code and running reproducible experiments, including data pipelines, forecasting models, optimization prototypes, and simulation studies.

Programming experience in Python or MATLAB is useful. Familiarity with power and energy modeling is a plus.
