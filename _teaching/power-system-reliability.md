This graduate course develops the methods used to assess power-system reliability in planning and operation. It connects probabilistic models with engineering decisions about generation, reserves, and interconnected systems.

Topics include adequacy, security and resilience; generation and load modeling; discrete convolution; Markov models; and Monte Carlo simulation. Operating studies connect spinning-reserve assessment with energy and reserve co-optimization. Interconnected-system studies cover multi-area reliability, boundary-state enumeration, decomposition, and composite-system assessment.

Worked examples and MATLAB exercises connect probabilistic calculations to planning and operating decisions, including how interconnection, reserve sharing, and renewable uncertainty affect reliability.
