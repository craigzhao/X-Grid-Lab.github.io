This graduate course develops the methods used to assess power-system reliability in planning and operation. It connects probabilistic models with engineering decisions about generation, reserves, and interconnected systems.

Topics include adequacy, security and resilience, generation and load modeling, discrete convolution, Markov models, Monte Carlo simulation, and composite-system reliability.
