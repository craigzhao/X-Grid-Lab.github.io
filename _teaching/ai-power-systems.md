This course connects artificial intelligence and advanced data analytics with power engineering. It introduces the measurements, models, and computational tools used to understand and operate modern power systems.

Topics include SCADA, PMU, and smart-meter data; convolutional and recurrent neural networks; graph neural networks; Transformers; physics-informed learning; and renewable and load forecasting. Hands-on work connects model development with data preparation, validation, and power-system applications, including time-series forecasting with PatchTST and TiDE.
