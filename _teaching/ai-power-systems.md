This course connects artificial intelligence and advanced data analytics with power engineering. It introduces the measurements, models, and computational tools used to understand and operate modern power systems.

Topics include power-system data sources, deep learning, graph neural networks, Transformers, physics-informed learning, and forecasting applications.

**Instructor:** Prof. Tianqiao Zhao  
**Listed offerings:** EE 5379, Section 003 (graduate), and EE 4328, Section 008 (undergraduate).
